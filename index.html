I'm a Data Science & AI graduate from Mumbai with one simple belief: data is a tool, insight is the impact. I love the quiet moment when a messy spreadsheet finally gives up its secret.

Right now, I'm working as a Data Analyst Intern at Houzeo, a US-based Proptech company, contributing to Project Houzeo India. My work centers on spatial data analysis and image analysis - turning complex geospatial patterns into actionable intelligence with Python scripts, AWS S3 storage systems, and Elasticsearch.

Away from the keyboard, I'm a Chess player, a Sketcher, a Yoga Therapist, and a traveler drawn to quiet waters and open trails - usually with a book nearby and coffee within reach.
